# Instructions

Read and follow **AGENTS.md** in the repository root. The essentials:

1. **Log every change** at the top of `modern/vt-modern-development-notes.md` (newest first):
   `### YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM ET — <who> — <short title>` with **What:**, **Why:**, **Files:**, **Commit:**,
   and optionally **Next person must know:**. Helper:
   `python3 modern/tools/log_dev_note.py --agent <who> --title "..." --what "..." --why "..."`.
   The pre-commit hook blocks commits without an entry (`bash modern/tools/setup_dev_hooks.sh` enables it).
2. **Pull before editing**; change files through Git, never by copying whole files over newer ones.
3. Never hand-edit `modern/CHANGELOG.md` (generated by `modern/tools/changelog.py`).
4. Label utility work: read `modern/tools/LABEL_UTILITY_NOTES.md` first.
5. Commands for the owner to paste on the Pi end with `; beep3`.
