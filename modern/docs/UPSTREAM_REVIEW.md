# Upstream review tracker

Twice a month Bob (Claude) reviews what official ViewTouch (github.com/ViewTouch/viewtouch, branch main)
changed and recommends what to bring over. Reviews are in `modern/docs/upstream-reviews/<date>.md`.
The fork no longer shares history with upstream, so anything worth having is copied by hand.
